🔩 Motherboard Parts

5 basic mobile PCB components

🟫 Resistor
A small component that limits or controls the flow of electric current in a circuit, protecting sensitive parts from too much power.
On mobile PCB: Controls current to LEDs, sensors & small ICs. Identified by colour bands or SMD codes (e.g. "103").
🔎 Eppadi kandupidippathu:
• Pin: Two pin erugum
• Center: Mala (black) alladhu Blue colour block
• Pin side: Two pin-laum mala (black) iron erugum, side-la vera colour erugum
🌀 Coil (Inductor)
A coiled wire component that stores energy in a magnetic field and smooths/filters current, especially for power supply lines.
On mobile PCB: Found near charging IC & power management IC. Often a small square/round metal-shielded block.
🔎 Eppadi kandupidippathu:
• Pin: Two pin erugum
• Center: Mala (black) colour block
• Pin side: Two pin-laum mala (black) iron erugum, side-laum mala (black) colour erugum
🔵 Condenser (Capacitor)
Stores and releases small amounts of electrical energy, used to smooth voltage and filter out noise from the power line.
On mobile PCB: The most common part — tiny black/tan SMD rectangles all over the board, near battery & IC pins.
🔎 Eppadi kandupidippathu:
• Pin: Two pin erugum
• Center: Mala brown colour erugum
🔺 Diode
Allows current to flow in only one direction, protecting the circuit from reverse current and converting AC to DC in some paths.
On mobile PCB: Used in charging circuits & battery protection paths. Small black component with a band marking direction.
🔎 Eppadi kandupidippathu:
• Pin: Two pin erugum
• Band: Adi-la (bottom side-la) oru kodu (line/band) erugum
⬛ IC (Integrated Circuit)
A chip that packs thousands of tiny circuits together to process signals, manage power, or control specific functions of the phone.
On mobile PCB: Includes CPU, power management IC (PMIC), charging IC, audio IC, Wi-Fi/RF IC — the "brains" of the board.
🔎 Eppadi kandupidippathu:
• Pin: Multiple pin (pala pin) erugum — board-la pin adhigama irundha adhu IC-thaan
